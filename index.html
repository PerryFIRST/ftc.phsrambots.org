About Us

We are Rambler Robotics, FTC Team 12337. Our team was established in 2016 and is based in Perry, Michigan. We're a small team of 4 students, along with a coach and a couple of mentors, who work diligently together on design, programming, and manufacturing. We support the environment by recycling pop cans and returning scrap metal to help raise funds for our travel and robot expenses. Our community outreach consists of free movie nights in our school auditorium, bottle drives, family fun festivals, volunteering at the local VFW hall, and inviting other local teams to workshops and events.

About FIRST

FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is a global nonprofit organization that designs accessible, innovative programs that motivate young people to pursue opportunities in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM).

Through FIRST programs, students engage in exciting challenges that build problem-solving and collaboration skills. Our FTC (FIRST Tech Competition) team designs and builds robots to compete against other teams in intense, high-energy matches. Every season brings new challenges, new learning opportunities, and the chance to showcase innovation and teamwork on a regional and national scale.

FIRST isn't just about building robots. It's about building the next generation of leaders, engineers, and innovators who will shape our future.

History

Since 2016, we have been working hard to build robots and compete in FIRST Tech Challenge events. As a small team, we've competed at FTC qualifiers around Michigan, learning and growing our skills in design, programming, and manufacturing along the way. As Rambler Robotics from Perry, Michigan, we strive to improve each season, pushing our limits while representing our school and community with pride.
