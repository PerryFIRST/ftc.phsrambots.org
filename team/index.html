About the Team

We are a team of 21, 7th - 12th grade students from Perry Michigan. We have a small team of mentors and coaches who help lead our students and guide them realize their full potential. Each student has a cruical role in making the robot work. Some of the things that our team members learn are CAD 3D modeling, how to properly use the CNC machine to manufacture parts, how to wire and program (code) the robot. We also have students who run the business aspects of our 501(c)(3). These students raise and manage funds, make scheduling, travel and food for competition decisions, as well as making schedules, scouting forms, and building up a following on social media.
