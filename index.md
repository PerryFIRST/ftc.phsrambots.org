# PMS RAMBLER ROBOTICS (phsrambots.org) - LLM Descriptor

## Overview

**PMS RAMBLER ROBOTICS** is the official website of the middle school robotics team based in Perry, Michigan. The team, known as **FIRST Tech Competition (FTC) Team 12337**, participates in competitive robotics while promoting STEM education, teamwork, and community engagement.

The site serves as a central hub for information about the team's activities, history, robots, competitions, and outreach efforts.

---

## Core Identity

* **Organization Type:** Middle school robotics team
* **Affiliation:** FIRST Tech Competition (FTC)
* **Team Number:** 12337
* **Location:** Perry, Michigan, USA
* **Founded:** 2016

---

## Key Content Areas

### 1. About the Team

The website provides an overview of the team's origins, growth, and mission:

* Started as a small rookie team and expanded over the years in size and skill
* Focus on collaboration, engineering, and real-world problem solving
* Emphasis on middle school student development in STEM fields

---

### 2. Robotics & Engineering

A major portion of the site highlights:

* Annual competition robots and iterations
* Technical features such as drive systems, mechanisms, and game-specific designs
* Performance summaries from competitions, including district, state, and world events

---

### 3. Competitions

The team competes in the **FIRST Tech Competition**, which involves:

* Designing and building robots for a new game each season
* Participating in FIRST in Michigan district events and the State Championship
* Qualifying for and attending world championships (multiple appearances)

---

### 4. Community Outreach

The site emphasizes community involvement, including:

* Mentoring the next generation of students
* Running workshops and collaborating with other teams
* Representing their school and community with pride

### 5. Sponsorship & Funding

The team is supported by:

* Local businesses and organizations
* Community donations
* Fundraising initiatives

### 6. Media & Engagement

The website links to:

* Match recordings and robot showcases
